Keel ↔ TackPath activity

    Test the whole operation, start to finish

    Everything runs in this browser tab: the real Keel app and server, and the real TackPath dispatcher, PathIQ and driver app. Nothing is sent anywhere. Start over (or reloading the page) resets everything.

    1. Online store: place a same-day order for a customer.
    2. Keel manager: watch it arrive and get released to the floor by itself.
    3. Picker: open the task, scan the bin, scan the item (use the Scanner panel).
    4. Packer: scan the order number, scan each item, weigh and seal, Print package labels, then Complete packing. Keel releases the delivery and writes its SmartSort file.
    5. Dispatcher: Keel's SmartSort file is waiting. Upload it to SmartSort, then assign the route to a driver. Keel follows the route by reading TackPath, and never writes to it.
    6. PathIQ: scan the Keel package label, open a bin (BIN, then LOC), scan the package again, then stage it (STG).
    7. Driver app: sign in (phone (404) 555-0161, the code arrives as a text), go to pickup, scan the bin and load the package, start the route, drive, deliver with a photo.
    8. Keel manager / Bolt portal: the order shows staged, shipped, out for delivery and delivered, with proof.
    9. Putaway (optional): on the Receiving handheld, receive BOLT-PO-77 onto DOCK-2, then open Put away. Bins have standard sizes: pick shelving A-01/A-02 is 24 × 24 × 15 in (200 lb), small-parts bins A-03 are 18 × 11 × 10 in (40 lb), pallet rack B is a 48 × 40 in pallet spot, 60 in high (2,500 lb). Keel counts what fits and splits the load when one bin can't take it all. Try scanning a full bin.
    10. Returns (optional): authorize an RMA in Manager › Returns, receive and inspect it on the Receiving handheld (Returns).
    11. Billing: every pick, pack, label and return is a billable event. Create an invoice draft for Bolt, issue it, record a payment. Bolt sees it in its portal.
