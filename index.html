What is TackPath?+

TackPath is logistics operations software for teams managing warehouse sorting and last-mile delivery. It combines manifest-based route planning, PathIQ scanning, a driver workflow, and a Dispatcher workspace for assignments, location updates, and delivery progress.

Does TackPath work with Shopify?+

Yes. Connect a retailer's Shopify store from your dispatch desk once, and new orders arrive on your dispatch board as same-day delivery jobs, with the address already mapped.

How do drivers join?+

Drivers in the Atlanta metro can apply on the Drive for TackPath page. Working drivers use the TackPath Driver app for their routes.

How does TackPath plan routes?+

Import a CSV with order IDs, recipients, addresses, and package quantities. SmartSort groups stops geographically and improves their sequence. Traffic-aware estimates help with planning; your team remains responsible for checking addresses, capacity, and delivery requirements.

Who is it built for?+

TackPath is built for courier companies and the teams running delivery operations. If you manage last-mile or 3PL delivery work, we can walk through your workflow and discuss whether the platform fits.

What does PathIQ do in the warehouse?+

PathIQ looks up a scanned package against active route manifests, displays its destination bin, and asks the operator to scan that bin to confirm placement. It is a focused route-sorting workflow; discuss your receiving, inventory, and warehouse requirements during a walkthrough.

How do we get started?+

Request a walkthrough or email hello@tackpath.com. Tell us about your delivery operation so we can focus the conversation on the workflows that matter to your team.
