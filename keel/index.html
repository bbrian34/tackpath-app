Warehouse

From the dock to the doorstep.

Keel covers the full warehouse workflow, with accuracy checks at every step so mistakes are caught before they ship.

Receiving & putaway

Receive exactly what was promised.

Clients send advance shipment notices. Your team receives against them by scan, and Keel directs every carton to a bin.

  • Receive against ASNs, with variances shown
  • Over-receipts refused before anything is counted
  • Directed putaway with suggested bins
  • Stock available to orders only once it is on a shelf
Fulfillment

Pick and pack with nothing left to chance.

Work goes to pickers in walking order, priority orders first. Every item is scanned again at the pack bench before the box is sealed.

  • Wave, batch and single-order picking on any handheld or scanner
  • Wrong bin or wrong item refused at the scan
  • Pack verification with carton weights
  • Parcel shipping or local delivery hand-off
Inventory

Inventory you can stand behind.

Every unit's movement is recorded with who did it and when, and stock on hand is audited against the ledger.

  • Available, reserved and on-dock quantities by bin
  • Pallets and license plates, broken down to the each
  • Returns to stock or quarantine
  • Low-stock alerts per client SKU
Clients & billing

Built for the business of a 3PL.

Give every client visibility, and bill them for exactly the work you did.

Client portal

A dedicated view for every client.

Each client signs in to see only its own inventory, inbound shipments, orders and invoices, live.

  • Create ASNs and orders, or send orders from an online store by API
  • Follow every order from release to delivery
  • Invoices with every charge traced to the work

Rate cards per client

Receiving, storage, picks, packing, materials, shipping and returns, each priced the way you sell them.

Captured as it happens

Billable events are recorded the moment the work is done, so nothing is missed at month end.

Invoices, taxes, payments

Generate invoices per client with taxes, and record payments against them.

Data kept apart

One client's inventory, orders and invoices are never visible to another.

Roles and accountability

Owners, managers, receivers, pickers, packers and billing staff each sign in as themselves.

Orders by API

Clients' stores send orders straight to Keel with their own API keys.

Same-day fulfillment

Same-day delivery that runs itself.

Set a cutoff, a delivery-by time and the ZIP codes you serve. Keel accepts or declines each order the moment it arrives and moves accepted orders through the floor on its own.

  • Every order checked against the address and the clock
  • Released to the floor ahead of everything else, automatically
  • Local delivery booked the moment the box is sealed
  • Late or out-of-area orders declined with the reason, next-day instead
