What Keel does

One system for the warehouse floor, the office and your merchants, built for 3PLs that store and ship for many brands at once.

Receiving

Receive by scan

Merchants send advance shipment notices. Receivers scan the shipment, the dock and each carton on a Zebra handheld. Over-receipts are refused and nothing is counted, so stock stays exact.

Putaway

From the dock to a shelf

Scan a carton and Keel suggests the bin. Scan the bin label to confirm. Stock becomes available to orders only once it is on a shelf.

Orders

Orders from every channel

Orders arrive from merchants' online stores through Keel's API with their own keys, from the merchant portal, or entered by staff. Stock is reserved the moment an order is accepted.

Picking

Right bin, right item

Pickers get their work on the handheld, in walking order, with same-day orders first. A wrong bin or a wrong item is refused before anything is picked.

Packing

Checked, weighed, sealed

Every item is scanned again at the bench. An item that is not in the order is refused. Boxes are weighed and sealed, then labeled or handed to local delivery.

Inventory

A ledger for every unit

Every movement is recorded: received, put away, picked, packed, shipped, returned. By whom and when, people and automation alike, and audited against stock on hand.

Containers

Pallets and license plates

Move pallets and cartons as one unit with license-plate numbers, or break them down to the each.

Returns

Returns and substitutions

Receive returns back into stock or quarantine. Merchants decide whether a picker may substitute an item, and Keel enforces it.

Billing

Bill every merchant correctly

Rate cards per merchant for receiving, storage, picks, packing, materials and shipping. Billable events are captured as the work happens and invoiced with taxes and payments.

How an order moves through Keel

The same path the film follows, on real Keel screens.

  1. ReceiveThe merchant's ASN meets the truck at the dock. Every carton is scanned in.
  2. Put awayKeel suggests the bin. A scan confirms it and the stock becomes available.
  3. OrderA customer checks out on the merchant's store. The order reaches Keel in seconds.
  4. PickThe order appears on the picker's handheld. Wrong bins are refused.
  5. PackEvery item is scanned again, weighed and sealed. Wrong items are refused.
  6. Ship or deliverA parcel label, or a same-day hand-off to local delivery, with no one pressing start.

Same-day delivery that runs itself

Set a cutoff, a delivery-by time and the ZIP codes your vans serve. Keel does the rest.

  • Each order is checked against the address and the clock the moment it arrives
  • Accepted same-day orders get a promise, for example delivered by 9 PM
  • Released to the floor automatically, ahead of everything else, with no manager click
  • When the box is sealed, Keel books local delivery by itself
  • Late or out-of-area orders are declined for same-day with the reason, and go out next day
  • Merchants and the warehouse see the same status: packed, shipped, delivered
